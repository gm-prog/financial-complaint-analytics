# Research references

These sources inform the report's treatment of complaint handling, response timeliness, data quality, Power BI modelling, and interpretation. They are contextual references; they do not establish that the supplied training/project data is a CFPB dataset or subject to CFPB requirements.

1. **International Organization for Standardization.** ISO 10002:2018 — *Quality management — Customer satisfaction — Guidelines for complaints handling in organizations.* Published July 2018; reviewed and confirmed in December 2023. The standard covers planning, operation, maintenance, and improvement of an organisational complaint-handling process.  
   https://www.iso.org/standard/10002

2. **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau.** *Your company's role in the complaint process.* CFPB Company Portal guidance says companies provide a response within 15 calendar days and, where that response is not final, may provide a final response within up to 60 calendar days. This is a US CFPB process reference only; the project dataset's 15-day due interval does not prove CFPB jurisdiction.  
   https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/consumer-complaint-program/company-process/

3. **Government Digital Service, UK Government.** *The Government Data Quality Framework: guidance.* Describes completeness, uniqueness, consistency, timeliness, validity, and accuracy as data-quality dimensions and recommends disclosing missing data and cleaning methods.  
   https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-government-data-quality-framework/the-government-data-quality-framework-guidance

4. **Microsoft Learn.** *Understand star schema and the importance for Power BI.* Describes fact and dimension roles, relationships, and filter/grouping versus summarisation responsibilities in a semantic model.  
   https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/guidance/star-schema

5. **Microsoft Learn.** *Design guidance for date tables in Power BI Desktop.* Explains unique, nonblank, continuous date values and the role of a date dimension in time-intelligence analysis.  
   https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/guidance/model-date-tables

6. **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau.** *The CFPB to Cease Discretionary Publication of Complaint Narratives and Visualizations.* 14 August 2026. The announcement notes that unverified complaint narratives are one-sided accounts and do not necessarily establish legal violations. This supports treating complaint categories as investigation signals rather than adjudicated findings.  
   https://www.consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/the-cfpb-to-cease-discretionary-publication-of-complaint-narratives-and-visualizations/

7. **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau.** *Learn how the complaint process works.* Summarises submission, routing to companies, company responses, publication, and consumer review in the CFPB process.  
   https://www.consumerfinance.gov/complaint/process/
